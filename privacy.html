DuelPack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24. Applies to the DuelPack Android app and the DuelPack Telegram Mini App.

What we collect

  • Game settings (sound, theme, difficulty and similar options). Stored on your device. In Telegram a copy is stored in Telegram CloudStorage tied to your Telegram account so settings survive app data clearing.
  • Telegram account data (Telegram only): your Telegram user ID, name and language as provided by Telegram in the Mini App launch data. Used only to identify purchases and verify requests to our bot server.
  • Purchases (Telegram Stars): item, amount, date and the Telegram payment ID. Used to deliver purchased items and handle refunds.

The Android app does not require an account, does not request any device permissions and does not send data to our servers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before release and the ad provider will be listed here.

Payments

Purchases in the Telegram Mini App are processed by Telegram (Telegram Stars). We never see your payment card details. For payment issues send /paysupport to the game bot.

Retention and deletion

Settings stay until you delete the app or clear Telegram data. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to deliver items and comply with law. To request deletion of your data, contact us via the game bot or the email below.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

Мы храним только настройки игры (на устройстве и, в Telegram, в CloudStorage вашего аккаунта), Telegram ID для покупок за Stars и записи о покупках. Android-версия не требует аккаунта, не запрашивает разрешений и ничего не отправляет на наши серверы. Данные не продаются и не передаются третьим лицам. Вопросы по оплате — команда /paysupport в боте игры; удаление данных — через бота или по email выше.
